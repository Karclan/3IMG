# 2nd Project

A potential 2nd project idea would be some sort of Action RPG - A diablo esq game. However it'd be setup like a survivors game - MegaBonk, Vampire Survivors ect. The genre might be a little over done but I think it could be interesting mechanically to setup something where the abilities are structured like they are in Path Of Exile.

Effectively in Path Of Exile - ever ability can have a number of support abilities linked into them. Those links then change and modify the ability in some way shape or form. 
There are basic modifications like speed, damage, cooldown, mana cost, but there are also more complex ones like projectile chaining, stacking charges of an ability to cast all at once, spawning an entity that fires that particular skill over and over, even changing what the cost of the skill is from mana to health or other resources. 

The mechanical depth is quite complex but with a good code infrastructure it could be very fun to make.

With the amount of visuals there I imagine there is a lot of space for fun art.

Audio and Music have a high amount of variety too and various supports could adjust the audio in unique ways.

Story? it can be anything we want it to be so Livy could go hog wild with whatever she wants. My initial thoughts would be something that's told through gameplay, whether that's level design, small snippets of lore given to the character, dialog from the player character or enemies, or even a narrator like seen in Bastion.
