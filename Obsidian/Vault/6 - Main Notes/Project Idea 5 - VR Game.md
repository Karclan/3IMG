I would like to explore the idea of a VR game

Why?
The games in the market are rarely good and we are extremely VR savvy.
The vast majority have terrible looking models, specially hands and awkward traversal
The good ones are usually story-driven/horror/puzzle games or high effort guns physics

I'd like us to make a game with the following restrictions:

-No magic
-No sword/shield combat
-No guns (maybe)

The point is that the combat shouldn't be the main focus. There will be better options out there.

Think of something that could be dynamic, so not a escape room, more like Portal or Alien Isolation. 

Must have an actually good story. Even the best games in this category rarely go a foot deep in story-telling. If we could make something like a Silent Hill or something that explore a multi-perspective subject it could become the first of it's kind.

Must plays:
-I expect you to die
-RED MATTER 2
-FNAF Help Wanted
-METRO Awakening
-Duck Season
-Budget Cuts
-Walking Dead Saints and Sinners
-Superhot VR (The original story was removed, but it's very inspiring)

The important bits:

-Good hand models with all the fucking movements and that feels good (personalization to hand)
-Fuck motion sickness, we ball.

**Another route:**
Something that has never been recreated after it died was the main lobby in Echo Arena, which was a social VR environment with people trying to help each other learn and play the game.

Maybe something like a dungeon crawler of sorts or chalice dungeons in VR with a social vr pre-lobby wouldn't be such a bad idea.

The way it worked is: Private lobbies do not exist. To go through the game you had to go trough a public lobby. The best you could do is change to a different public lobby.

I wouldn't like to make a fantasy VR game. I think it should be sci-fi or modern.
Fantasy settings are harder to make the player feel immersed and even harder not to fall for the magic sword combat tropes. I would prefer if we made darker settings.
